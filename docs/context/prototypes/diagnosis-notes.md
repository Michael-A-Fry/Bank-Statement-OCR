WHY 0 OF 80 (plain terms): the reader assumes a file holds one transaction table. In these packs it takes every line that has a date and an amount, on every page, as a row of the statement. That sweeps in pending items, loan schedules, rate and fee tables, account summaries and other accounts' mini-statements. The columns it builds are a mix of all those tables, so the balance never adds up. Nothing proves, so nothing goes through automatically. The checks did their job: nothing wrong got through. Four smaller causes add to this: (1) any page with a table of figures counts as a "transactions page"; (2) "Opening", "Closing" and "Total" lines are taken from anywhere in the pack; (3) words like "Cash advance (credit card)" in a fee schedule make 36 of the 72 everyday accounts look like credit cards, so two sign conventions both fit; (4) three wordings in the statement itself are not recognised: "Balance at start of period" (14 packs), "Total movements" (7 packs), and a second account that opens with "Balance brought forward" (1 pack).
